RESET and INJECT are deliberately empty for this bench. It answers "what does this machine have for the packaged-installer boundary?" and must not create, seed or tear down anything to answer it.

The probe is an inventory, not a readiness verdict: it always reports `readiness.status = not_established` because account access, hook trust, disposable test isolation and Store authority cannot be observed from files and version commands.

## Verdict

The instrument covers exactly the Step 1.1 inventory boundary: one observation-only probe plus the document that says how to read it. It is honestly red on two observed gaps (Cursor and Copilot are not installed); Codex and Antigravity are installed and unconfigured, which is reported separately as the expected first-run state. Readiness is `not_established` by design — hardware, accounts and Store authority are human inputs, and the reserved Store identity belongs to Step 3.2/6.2, not to a development-identity Step 1.2 probe. Fixtures are tribal (inside the product unittest suite) and no per-feature checklist exists yet; both belong to later phases, not to this bench.
